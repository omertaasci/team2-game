# 🎄 Christmas Quest: Save Christmas!
### 2D Multiplayer Co-op RPG

## 1. Game Concept

Christmas Quest is a small 2D multiplayer cooperative RPG where 2–6 players work together to save Christmas.

The game takes place in a small snowy Christmas town with approximately 5–10 buildings. There is also a mysterious cave outside the town where the final boss lives.

### Story

An evil Ice King has stolen the Christmas presents from the town and hidden them inside his cave.

The players arrive in the village and meet five NPCs who need their presents back. Each NPC explains what was stolen and asks the players to recover it.

Players explore the town, talk to NPCs, collect information about the stolen presents and travel together to the cave.

Inside the cave, they face the Ice King in a cooperative boss fight. After defeating him, they recover the presents and return them to the NPCs to complete their quests and save Christmas.

## 2. Game World

**Christmas Town**
- A small snowy village
- Five NPCs with individual quests.
- Players spawn in the town.

**Ice Cave**
- Located outside the town.
- Contains the final boss,
- Players fight the boss together.
- Stolen presents are placed inside the cave.

## 3. Characters

### Playable Characters
1. **Warrior** – Strong melee attacks and high health.
2. **Mage** – Uses magic attacks.
3. **Archer** – Attacks from a distance.
4. **Healer** – Can restore teammates' health.

### NPCs
1. **Santa** – Gives the main quest.
2. **Elf** – Lost his gift.
3. **Toy Maker** – Needs his stolen presents.
4. **Snowman** – Wants his magical snow crystal back.
5. **Reindeer Keeper** – Needs a special gift for his reindeer.

### Boss
6. **Ice King** – The only enemy in the game. He stole all the presents and waits inside the cave.

## 4. Main Gameplay Features

- Real-time multiplayer for 2–6 players.
- Character selection.
- WASD movement.
- Player-to-player synchronization.
- NPC interaction and quests.
- Five collectible Christmas presents.
- One cave and one boss fight.
- Basic health and damage system.
- Quest completion and rewards.
- In-game chat(maybe).

## 5. Technology Stack

- **Game Engine:** Phaser 3
- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- **Backend:** Node.js
- **Multiplayer:** Socket.IO

## 6. Final Goal

Deliver a playable multiplayer Christmas RPG where players can explore a snowy town together, interact with five NPCs, travel to a cave, defeat the Ice King and return the stolen presents.

Our priority is functional multiplayer gameplay rather than complex graphics or advanced RPG mechanics.
